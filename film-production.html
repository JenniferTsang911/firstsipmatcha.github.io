Venue/ Production Requirements

To ensure the best experience for your production, we require the following from the venue:

  • Power Access: Access to a dedicated standard power outlet within 10 feet of the setup area.
  • Space: A minimum 6ft x 4ft flat surface for our bar setup.
  • Access: Load-in access 60 minutes before the service start time.
